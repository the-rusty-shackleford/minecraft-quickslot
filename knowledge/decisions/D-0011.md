# D-0011: Keep Quick Slot distinct when a backpack covers the back

Accepted 2026-09-18. Rusty selected: "Small items at hip; large items hidden" when
Backpacks+ is visibly worn, instead of adding a fifth visual attachment to the bag.
The bag's independent 2/3/4 mount cells, H behavior and G exchanges remain unchanged.

Back-anchored Quick Slot items use the pocket verse while a visible Backpacks+ bag is
worn. Lower-back items move to the hip by default. Hidden Curios equipment, bags carried
in hand and bags merely in inventory do not cover the back. Normal placement returns
when the worn bag is hidden or removed. Bowl meals remain always hidden; elytra hides
all Quick Slot displays. Light and storage are independent of this visibility policy.

Use a guarded client-only callback from the companion's existing equipment snapshot.
No new inventory scan, per-frame allocation, packet, other-mod mixin or hard dependency
is required. Resource `with_backpack` transforms apply to visible hip items after their
ordinary transform. They do not bypass the back/bowl/elytra hiding rules.

The initial real-client capture showed that the original rear hip was inside the bag's
lower edge. In backpack mode only, lower the item and its carrying loop by 0.18 blocks
so it sits beneath that edge. Keep the approved no-backpack scale and transforms intact.
Remove the obsolete unused sling/depth/pushout settings from the abandoned SB design;
keep the configurable lower-back-to-hip switch. Release remains held.
