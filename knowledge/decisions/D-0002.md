# D-0002 — Driving controls take priority over Quick Slot

Accepted 2026-09-17 from Rusty's correction during Phase 1.

H is the default Quick Slot swap key. When the player controls a Vanilla Wheels vehicle,
Quick Slot's conflict context is inactive and its client/server swap paths reject the
action. H continues through Vanilla Wheels' existing light key. A passenger is not a
driver and retains Quick Slot. Rebinding remains available in Controls.

Vehicle references are isolated behind a ModList guard, with a compile-only dependency;
Quick Slot must still load without Vanilla Wheels. No changes to Vanilla Wheels are needed.
Remove Stowed from isolated test profiles because the two mods overlap. This instruction
does not authorize publishing a new pack or changing the production instance yet.
