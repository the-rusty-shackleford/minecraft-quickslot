# D-0008: Move backpack development to Backpacks+

Rusty requested `minecraft-backpacks-plus` on 2026-09-17, named Backpacks+, and directed
the backpack work there with an eventual migration away from Sophisticated Backpacks.

For the new backpack, tools/weapons should mount on its sides and small items on its
outer face or hanging from it. This supersedes the original behind-the-pack presentation
target for Backpacks+. The source of mounted gear (Quick Slot, explicit mount slots or
selected backpack contents) is still being clarified.

Quick Slot retains the approved normal body fit, permanent bowl-meal hiding and complete
elytra hiding. The interim 80% Sophisticated Backpacks resources remain until the migration;
do not spend further effort on its pushout/clearance implementation.

Rusty's release request was followed by their explicit instruction to complete remaining
features first. No repository, tag, release, pack update or restart was performed. Quick Slot
remains unreleased; its dynamic-light, configuration and compatibility work is still pending.
The backpack phase now depends on the Backpacks+ design and its eventual integration.

The release audit also identified Stowed's serialized `stowed:holster` ItemStack attachment.
Replacing Stowed must preserve any such item; no migration code has been written yet.
