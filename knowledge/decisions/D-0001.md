# D-0001 — Server-owned quick slot with swap interaction

Accepted 2026-09-17 from Rusty's full brief and nine decision answers.

Use a serialized `AttachmentType<ItemStack>` with `ItemStack.OPTIONAL_CODEC` on each player.
Only the stack persists; revisions and packet-rate state are session-local. Copy stacks at
ownership boundaries so a slot, hotbar and clone cannot share a mutable stack accidentally.

A required versioned payload protocol makes both sides mandatory. Client intents carry an
index and expected revision, never item data. Server mutations send snapshots to tracking
players and the owner. Lifecycle/tracking events bootstrap observers without per-tick sync.
Render classification never determines whether an item may be stored.

The selected interaction is swap, avoiding invasive hand/selection mixins and preserving
Create/Controlify's normal hotbar semantics. The inventory menu extension comes in Phase 2.
Successful swaps get a brief HUD pulse rather than pretending a tenth slot stays selected.

KeepInventory and the server keep-on-death option retain the stack, including Vanishing,
matching vanilla retention precedence. Otherwise the normal LivingDropsEvent owns the
drop; Vanishing removes the item, and death clones remain empty. End-return cloning copies
the remaining stack defensively and never adds to a previously copied stack.

Later body presentation keeps items visible with elytra, adds visible torches/lanterns,
uses one displayed item, and prioritizes a natural body-relative scale. Wide items move to
the hip with a backpack. Any SB mixin requires review of its exact hook before implementation.
