# D-0007: Worn Sophisticated Backpacks use the approved 80% scale

Rusty approved the 80% comparison on 2026-09-17: "The 80% looks great."
They plan a separate custom backpack mod and asked not to spend much effort refining
Sophisticated Backpacks.

Use uniform 0.8 scale for its worn backpack models. Keep the exact rotation and translation
from the approved preview. This includes the small mounting gap visible while crouching;
do not expand this into another clearance or offset pass.

Implement the cosmetic adjustment with six item-model resource overrides, one per installed
tier, inheriting Sophisticated Backpacks' original block models. Only the
`sophisticatedbackpacks:worn` display transform changes. Model artwork, GUI/held/placed
transforms, storage capacity and Quick Slot body placements retain their existing behavior.
No Sophisticated Backpacks source or jar is modified, and no foreign-mod mixin is needed.
Active resource packs can replace these models in the normal resource order.

This is a small interim cosmetic adjustment, not completion of Quick Slot's backpack
layering phase. Curios visibility, quick-slot items behind the pack and wide-item handling
remain separate work. Releases and production modpack changes remain HELD.
