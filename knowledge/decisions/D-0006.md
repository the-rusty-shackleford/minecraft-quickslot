# D-0006: Restore the earlier fit; always hide bowl meals and elytra displays

Accepted from Rusty's gallery feedback on 2026-09-17 and their explicit follow-up that
bowl meals are always in the "pocket verse".

The gallery's Earlier model checks are the preferred reference. Restore the exact default
placements from Phase 3 (0cfea66): back center (0, 0.32), measured surface depth 0.135,
30-degree 3D-tool sling, 75-degree sprite sling, 22-degree trident sling, and centered shields.
Keep the earlier model normalization, scales and belt anchors. Refined Tools still supplies
the active models. Manual resource placement remains available for unusual items.

Accept minor hair/helmet clipping, including looking all the way up. Remove automatic
armor/head clearance offsets, head collision tests and pose-triggered hiding. This
supersedes D-0005's head-obstruction fallback and the original strict anti-clipping requirement.

Bowl meals are always hidden on the body, with or without elytra. Bowl style tags, crafting
remainders and food conversion remainders identify them. Placement overrides cannot force
a recognized bowl meal back onto the belt. An empty bowl is an ordinary component.

Wearing elytra hides every quick-slot body display: back tools, belt tools, food, potions,
torches, lanterns and all other categories. There is no elytra alternate position. Removing
elytra restores the ordinary display, except for independently hidden categories such as
bowl meals. This supersedes D-0005's narrow-tool rear-hip carry.

These decisions affect body rendering only. Storage, H swaps, server authority and future
dynamic light remain independent. Cape/backpack rules not superseded here retain their
prior intent. Releases remain HELD.
