# D-0010: Preserve Stowed holsters during the approved legacy retirement

Accepted 2026-09-18 under the authorized remaining-work pass. Backpacks+ D-0019 records
Rusty's explicit discard of legacy backpack contents; it does not discard the separate
Stowed holster stack. No free replacement backpacks are granted.

Use an offline development-only preparation tool to rename `stowed:holster` to
`quickslot:stack` in copied native player NBT. Preserve the complete raw item and every
unrelated field, refuse an occupied destination, and never write the source directory.
This avoids a permanent production attachment alias or another mod's mixin. The copied
outputs are reviewable, checksummed, and idempotent. The actual release requires a fresh
stopped-server copy and a consistent rollback backup; the early audit snapshot is not
used directly for deployment. All real player data remains private and local.

See `validation/legacy-cutover.md` for the completed rehearsal and held cutover procedure.
This decision does not authorize publication, deployment or a production restart.
